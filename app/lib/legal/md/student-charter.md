## Your Privileges, Obligations & What to Expect

## PART A — WHAT NAADVIDYA OFFERS STUDENTS

### A1. Access to curated teachers
- Every teacher on Naadvidya has been personally reviewed and approved by the Naadvidya Gurus
- You are not choosing from an open marketplace — you are choosing from a vetted faculty
- Teacher profiles include qualifications, specialisations, ragas taught, teaching language, and session fee
- Intro videos (where available) let you hear and see the teacher before booking

### A2. Three learning formats (Phase 1 and beyond)

**Mehfil Session (available now)**
On-demand sessions with any approved teacher. You book, they confirm, you learn. No long-term commitment. Perfect for testing a new teacher or supplementing existing learning.

**Riyaaz Workshop (Phase 1.5)**
Fixed-duration, topic-focused workshops: "Yaman in 8 sessions", "Thumri Aesthetics", etc. You know exactly what you're getting and when it ends. Ideal for targeted skill-building.

**Gurukul Path (Phase 1.5)**
Full progressive curriculum — from where you are now to where you want to be. Weekly sessions, structured assignments, monthly progress reviews, and a teacher who knows your journey deeply.

### A3. Homework and feedback loop
- After every session, your teacher may post an assignment
- You submit your practice via voice recordings and written notes — directly on the platform
- Your teacher reviews and responds with text or voice feedback before the next session
- This async loop is the core of Naadvidya's learning model — use it fully

### A4. Your own session room
- Every session has a private video room generated specifically for you and your teacher
- No third-party apps, no Zoom downloads, no "share this link" — everything is inside Naadvidya
- Your room is available 15 minutes before your session start time

## PART B — STUDENT OBLIGATIONS

### B1. Punctuality
- Please join your session within 5 minutes of the scheduled start time
- If you are more than 15 minutes late without prior notice, the teacher may close the room. The session credit is not refunded in this case.
- If you know you will be late, message your teacher through the platform in advance

### B2. Practice and preparation
- Naadvidya teachers are Sangeet Visharads and accomplished artists — come prepared
- Review your previous assignment and teacher feedback before each session
- Bring your tanpura (physical or app-based), notebook, and a quiet space

### B3. Respect
- Treat your teacher as a Guru. Indian Classical music is a lineage tradition — that relationship is sacred
- Conduct yourself with dignity during sessions and in all platform communications
- Inappropriate conduct, harassment, or disrespect toward any teacher or staff results in immediate account suspension

### B4. Communication through the platform
- Please keep all session-related communication within Naadvidya
- Do not ask teachers for their personal contact details for teaching purposes
- If you have an urgent issue, contact support@naadvidya.in

### B5. Cancellations
- If you cannot attend a session, cancel at least 48 hours in advance for a full credit refund
- Repeated no-shows (3+ in 60 days) will be flagged and your account may be reviewed by the Naadvidya Gurus

## PART C — STUDENT PRIVILEGES

### C1. Trial before commitment
- If you are uncertain about a teacher, book a single Mehfil Session before enrolling in a long programme
- You are never required to commit to a full curriculum without experiencing the teacher first

### C2. Switching teachers
- You may switch teachers at any time for Mehfil Sessions — simply book a different teacher
- For Gurukul Path, a formal switch request may be submitted. The Naadvidya Gurus review and approve within 48 hours
- If the switch is due to documented conduct issues with the teacher, full credit balance is protected

### C3. Pausing a programme
- Gurukul Path students may pause their enrolment once per term for up to 4 weeks
- A pause request requires 5-day advance notice
- Credits reserved for paused weeks are returned to your wallet

### C4. Raising concerns
- You may raise a concern about any session within 48 hours of the session date
- Concerns are handled by the Naadvidya Gurus personally in Phase 1
- For sensitive matters (conduct, harassment), email directly: amee@naadvidya.in — marked confidential

### C5. Notes and recordings
- All homework submissions (your voice recordings, notes) are stored privately in your account
- You may access and download your own submission files at any time
- Session recordings (if mutually consented) are stored privately in your folder
