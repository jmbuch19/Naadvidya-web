# NAADVIDYA — Teacher Terms & Conditions
## Version 1.0 | Requires legal review before publication

---

## PART A — JOINING & ELIGIBILITY

### A1. Who may apply as a teacher
- Any individual with a recognised Indian Classical music qualification (minimum Madhyama Pratham or equivalent) may apply
- Sangeet Visharad, Sangeet Alankar, or equivalent post-graduate qualifications are strongly preferred
- Instrumentalists (Tabla, Sitar, Sarod, Bansuri, Veena, Harmonium, etc.) with demonstrable teaching experience may apply
- Applicants from international backgrounds (NRI, diaspora, foreign nationals) are welcome

### A2. Approval process
- Submitting a profile does not guarantee approval
- The Naadvidya Gurus review all applications personally
- Approval criteria: qualifications, teaching experience, communication quality, profile completeness
- The Naadvidya Gurus may interview applicants via video call before approval
- Rejected applications receive written feedback and may be reconsidered after 90 days
- Approval is for the platform as a whole — individual class offerings are also subject to approval

### A3. Profile requirements (mandatory before going live)
- Professional photo (clear, well-lit, appropriate for an academic platform)
- Written bio (minimum 150 words) in English. Hindi/Gujarati optional additional
- Sangeet qualifications listed with year and institution
- Specialisations (ragas, taal, genre, instrument)
- Intro video (strongly recommended, not mandatory) — maximum 3 minutes
- Published availability slots (minimum 4 slots per week to remain active)

## PART B — REVENUE & PAYMENTS

### B1. Revenue share
- Teachers receive **80% of the effective session fee** for every completed session
- The platform owner (owner_admin, `is_owner = true`) receives 100% — no platform cut applies to their teaching sessions
- The platform retains 20% to cover operations, infrastructure, and Razorpay fees
- The 80% is calculated on the effective per-session rate as derived from the credit pack the student purchased

### B2. When teachers get paid
- Naadvidya operates a **manual payout model in Phase 1**
- Payouts are processed by Naadvidya on the **1st and 15th of every month**
- Teachers must have a valid UPI ID or bank account registered in their dashboard
- Minimum payout threshold: ₹500. Amounts below this roll over to the next cycle
- Payout records are visible in the teacher's Earnings dashboard at all times

### B3. Session fee setting
- Teachers set their own per-session fee in their profile
- Minimum fee: ₹500 per session
- Recommended range: ₹700–₹1,500 for Madhyama/Visharad; ₹1,000–₹2,500 for Alankar & Beyond
- Naadvidya may advise fee adjustments to maintain platform consistency — advice is not mandatory
- Fee changes take effect for new bookings only; existing confirmed sessions are honoured at the original fee

### B4. What teachers are never paid for
- Cancelled sessions (by teacher)
- No-show sessions (by teacher)
- Sessions disputed and resolved against the teacher
- Sessions cancelled by mutual agreement before the 24-hour window

## PART C — TEACHING OBLIGATIONS

### C1. Session quality standards
- Sessions must begin within 5 minutes of the scheduled start time
- Teachers must be in a quiet, adequately lit environment
- A tanpura or electronic shruti box must be available during vocal sessions
- Teachers are expected to have their curriculum or session plan ready before joining
- The platform may, with prior notice, audit a session recording (if recording is enabled) for quality assurance

### C2. Homework and assignments
- Teachers are strongly encouraged (not mandated) to post an assignment after every session
- Assignments posted must be reviewed within 72 hours of student submission
- Teachers who consistently fail to review homework within 72 hours will receive a dashboard warning
- Feedback must be constructive. One-word feedback ("Good.") is not acceptable and is grounds for a quality flag

### C3. Communication with students
- All student communication must happen within the Naadvidya platform (in-app messaging, assignment notes)
- Teachers must not share personal phone numbers, WhatsApp, email, or social media with students for teaching purposes
- This rule protects both teachers and students. Violations result in account suspension
- Emergency contact via platform support is allowed

### C4. Conduct
- Teachers must maintain professional conduct at all times
- Discrimination, harassment, or inappropriate content of any kind results in immediate suspension and permanent ban
- Political, religious, or commercially promotional content during sessions is prohibited
- Teachers may not promote competing platforms or their own independent teaching services to Naadvidya students

## PART D — AVAILABILITY & RELIABILITY

### D1. Minimum availability
- Active teachers must maintain at least **4 bookable slots per week**
- Dropping below 4 slots for more than 2 consecutive weeks triggers a profile review
- Teachers who are unavailable for more than 4 consecutive weeks (without prior notice to Naadvidya) will have their profile unlisted

### D2. Holiday and leave
- Teachers must mark holidays at least 7 days in advance (see Scheduling & Holiday Policy)
- Extended leave (> 2 weeks) must be communicated to Naadvidya at least 14 days prior
- All enrolled students must be notified of extended leave by the platform

### D3. Cancellation limits
- Maximum 2 session cancellations per month per student relationship
- Maximum 8 cancellations per month total (across all students)
- Exceeding these limits triggers a formal review by the Naadvidya Gurus

## PART E — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### E1. Curriculum and notes
- All original teaching materials (bandishes, notations, compositions) created by teachers remain the teacher's intellectual property
- Teachers grant Naadvidya a non-exclusive, non-commercial licence to display their profile, bio, and sample materials on the platform
- Teachers may not upload copyrighted materials (published books, recordings owned by others) to the platform

### E2. Session recordings
- Sessions may only be recorded with the explicit mutual consent of both teacher and student
- Recordings are stored in the student's private R2 folder — not publicly accessible
- Teachers may not record sessions on their own devices without student consent

---

## PART F — TERMINATION

### F1. Teacher-initiated departure
- Teachers may deactivate their profile at any time with 30 days written notice to Naadvidya
- All confirmed sessions within those 30 days must be honoured or rescheduled
- Enrolled students (Gurukul Path, Phase 1.5) must be given a transition plan

### F2. Platform-initiated removal
Naadvidya may suspend or remove a teacher's profile for:
- 3+ strikes within 90 days (no-shows, cancellations)
- Violation of conduct standards
- Teaching independently to Naadvidya students outside the platform (within 12 months of joining)
- Providing false qualification information

### F3. Pending payouts on termination
- All completed session payouts due are honoured regardless of the reason for termination
- Payouts for disputed sessions are held pending resolution
