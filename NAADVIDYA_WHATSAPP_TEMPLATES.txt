WHAT TO DO IF A TEMPLATE IS REJECTED BY META
============================================================

Common rejection reasons and fixes:

1. "Promotional content" — Remove any pricing, offers, or marketing language.
   Fix: reword to purely informational.

2. "Variable mismatch" — Variables must be sequential ({{1}}, {{2}}, not {{1}}, {{3}}).
   Fix: renumber all variables.

3. "URL not matching" — Button URL domain must match your verified business domain.
   Fix: ensure naadvidya.in is verified in Meta Business Manager before submitting.

4. "Template too similar to existing" — Meta rejects near-duplicates.
   Fix: differentiate wording meaningfully.

5. "Header and body too similar" — Header must add information, not repeat body.
   Fix: make header a label, not a summary.

If rejected: fix and resubmit. Do not create a new template with a different name
to bypass rejection — Meta tracks this and may restrict your account.
